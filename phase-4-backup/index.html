About OBUMSJ

Our Mission & Vision

ODA BULTUM UNIVERSITY MUSLIM STUDENTS' Jama'a (OBUMSJ) is dedicated to supporting Muslim students in their academic and spiritual journey. Our organization provides various services and programs to help students thrive in their university experience while maintaining their Islamic values.

We aim to create a supportive community where students can grow spiritually, academically, and socially while upholding to teachings of Islam.

Our Vision

To be a leading Muslim student community that fosters spiritual growth, academic excellence, and social responsibility, preparing members to become ethical leaders who contribute positively to society.

Our Mission

To provide a supportive environment for Muslim students to practice their faith, excel in their studies, and engage in community service through various programs, activities, and sectors that address their spiritual, academic, and social needs.

Our Sectors

Explore our 10 dedicated sectors serving the Muslim student community

Qirat

Teaching and promoting the beautiful recitation of the Holy Quran through specialized programs and competitions.

  • Quran Recitation Classes
  • Tajweed Lessons
  • Quran Competition
  • Memorization Programs

Dawah

Spreading the message of Islam through various outreach programs, seminars, and community engagement.

  • Islamic Seminars
  • Interfaith Dialogues
  • Campus Outreach
  • Islamic Literature

Social Affairs

Addressing social issues and providing support to students through counseling and community services.

  • Student Counseling
  • Community Service
  • Social Events
  • Support Programs

Academy

Educational programs and Islamic studies for students to enhance their knowledge and skills.

  • Islamic Courses
  • Skill Development
  • Workshops
  • Academic Support

Finance

Managing organization funds, donations, and financial activities to support various programs.

  • Fund Management
  • Budget Planning
  • Financial Reports
  • Donation Tracking

Ziyara

Organizing visits to Islamic historical sites and facilitating spiritual journeys for students.

  • Historical Site Visits
  • Spiritual Retreats
  • Educational Tours
  • Cultural Exchange

Rukiya

Providing spiritual healing through Quranic recitation and Islamic practices for wellbeing.

  • Quranic Healing
  • Spiritual Counseling
  • Dua Programs
  • Wellbeing Sessions

Charity

Managing charitable activities, zakat distribution, and support for needy students and community.

  • Zakat Distribution

  • Student Support
