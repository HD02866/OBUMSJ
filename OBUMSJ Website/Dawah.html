Key Dawah Messages

Peace & Compassion

Islam teaches peace, compassion, and mercy for all of creation. The very word "Islam" comes from the root word "Salaam" which means peace.

وَإِن جَنَحُوا لِلسَّلْمِ فَاجْنَحْ لَهَا وَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى اللَّهِ ۚ إِنَّهُ هُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ

Knowledge & Wisdom

Islam encourages seeking knowledge and using wisdom in all matters. The first word revealed in the Quran was "Iqra" which means "Read".

يَرْفَعِ اللَّهُ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا مِنكُمْ وَالَّذِينَ أُوتُوا الْعِلْمَ دَرَجَاتٍ

Justice & Equality

Islam establishes justice and equality for all human beings regardless of race, ethnicity, or social status. All are equal before Allah.

يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ إِنَّا خَلَقْنَاكُم مِّن ذَكَرٍ وَأُنثَىٰ وَجَعَلْنَاكُمْ شُعُوبًا وَقَبَائِلَ لِتَعَارَفُوا

Love & Brotherhood

Muslims are brothers and sisters to one another. The bond of faith is stronger than any other bond of kinship or nationality.

إِنَّمَا الْمُؤْمِنُونَ إِخْوَةٌ فَأَصْلِحُوا بَيْنَ أَخَوَيْكُمْ
