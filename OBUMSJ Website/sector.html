Our Dedicated Sectors

OBUMSJ operates through 10 specialized sectors, each focusing on different aspects of spiritual, academic, and social development for Muslim students.

Qirat

Teaching and promoting the beautiful recitation of the Holy Quran through specialized programs and competitions.

Key Features:

  • Quran Recitation Classes
  • Tajweed Lessons
  • Quran Competition
  • Memorization Programs

Objectives:

To enhance Quranic recitation skills among students, promote proper Tajweed, and encourage Quran memorization through structured programs and competitions.

Learn More About Qirat

Dawah

Spreading the message of Islam through various outreach programs, seminars, and community engagement.

Key Features:

  • Islamic Seminars
  • Interfaith Dialogues
  • Campus Outreach
  • Islamic Literature

Objectives:

To effectively convey the message of Islam to the university community, address misconceptions, and promote Islamic values through various outreach initiatives.

Learn More About Dawah

Social Affairs

Addressing social issues and providing support to students through counseling and community services.

Key Features:

  • Student Counseling
  • Community Service
  • Social Events
  • Support Programs

Objectives:

To provide social support to Muslim students, address their social concerns, and foster a sense of community through various social programs and counseling services.

Learn More About Social Affairs

Academy

Educational programs and Islamic studies for students to enhance their knowledge and skills.

Key Features:

  • Islamic Courses
  • Skill Development
  • Workshops
  • Academic Support

Objectives:

To provide comprehensive Islamic education, enhance academic performance, and develop essential skills for Muslim students through structured courses and workshops.

Learn More About Academy

Finance

Managing organization funds, donations, and financial activities to support various programs.

Key Features:

  • Fund Management
  • Budget Planning
  • Financial Reports
  • Donation Tracking

Objectives:

To ensure transparent and efficient financial management of the organization, proper allocation of resources, and accountability for all financial activities.

Learn More About Finance

Ziyara

Organizing visits to Islamic historical sites and facilitating spiritual journeys for students.

Key Features:

  • Historical Site Visits
  • Spiritual Retreats
  • Educational Tours
  • Cultural Exchange

Objectives:

To organize meaningful visits to Islamic historical and religious sites, facilitate spiritual growth, and promote cultural understanding among Muslim students.

Learn More About Ziyara

Rukiya

Providing spiritual healing through Quranic recitation and Islamic practices for wellbeing.

Key Features:

  • Quranic Healing
  • Spiritual Counseling
  • Dua Programs
  • Wellbeing Sessions

Objectives:

To provide spiritual healing and support through Quranic recitation, authentic Islamic practices, and counseling for students facing spiritual challenges.

Learn More About Rukiya

Charity

Managing charitable activities, zakat distribution, and support for needy students and community.

Key Features:

  • Zakat Distribution
  • Food Drives
  • Student Support
  • Community Aid

Objectives:

To effectively manage charitable activities, distribute zakat and sadaqah to deserving recipients, and provide material support to needy students and community members.

Learn More About Charity

Dawrul Hasen

Promoting continuous good deeds and sustainable charitable projects for long-term impact.

Key Features:

  • Sadaqah Jariyah
  • Sustainable Projects
  • Community Development
  • Long-term Impact

Objectives:

To establish sustainable charitable projects that continue to benefit the community, promote continuous good deeds, and create lasting positive impact.

Learn More About Dawrul Hasen

Information

Managing communication, media presence, and information dissemination for the organization.

Key Features:

  • Media Management
  • News Updates
  • Social Media
  • Public Relations

Objectives:

To effectively manage organizational communication, maintain a positive media presence, and ensure accurate and timely information dissemination to members and the public.

Learn More About Information
