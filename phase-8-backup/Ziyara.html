Ziyara Sector

The Ziyara Sector is responsible for Quran recitation programs, Tajweed training, and organizing competitions to enhance Quranic skills among members.

About Ziyara Sector

This department focuses on teaching correct Quran recitation, promoting memorization, and nurturing students to become skilled Qaris who represent the community in national and international competitions.
